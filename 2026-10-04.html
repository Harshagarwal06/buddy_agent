TechCrunch AI · Oct 3, 2026
Amazon responds to data center backlash, says it no longer uses NDAs Recommended

Amazon Web Services has ceased using nondisclosure agreements (NDAs) in its dealings with government agencies for data center approvals. This policy change comes as CEO Matt Garman addresses widespread suspicion and backlash against data centers, which have faced criticism for lack of transparency, water usage, and electricity costs. By eliminating NDAs, Amazon aims to counter over 100 data center moratoriums being considered across the U.S. and prevent what it calls a "losing ticket" for the country's technological future.
