TechCrunch AI · Nov 22, 2026
OpenAI’s chief scientist has thoughts on how to limit AI development Must read

OpenAI's chief scientist, Jakub Pachocki, has proposed using "perplexity" as a standard measurement to limit AI development. Perplexity quantifies how well a model predicts the next token, with lower scores indicating better predictions and correlating with advanced capabilities like chain-of-thought reasoning and transformative applications in cybersecurity and mathematics. This metric could serve as a shared benchmark for general intelligence, enabling AI companies to establish common limits on progress until understanding and monitoring AI behavior reach concrete safety milestones.

Why data centers are at the center of the Big Tech backlash Must read

Journalist Paris Marx's new book "Hyperscale" investigates the growing political backlash against data centers, which have become a significant point of contention amidst broader anger at the tech industry. Marx argues that the physical presence and environmental impact of these facilities, including their substantial energy and water consumption and noise pollution, directly affect local communities and their quality of life. This tangible local impact makes data centers an obvious target for public opposition, contributing to recent political shifts and signaling a wider movement against the physical manifestations of technology.
