Google thinks SpaceX’s Starship has to launch 1,800 times before space data centers get off the ground Must read

Google has launched a prototype orbital compute satellite carrying its Tensor Processing Unit (TPU) into space, marking the first time the tech giant has sent one of its advanced chips beyond Earth. Built by Planet Labs, the satellite will test the TPU's functionality in space, including continuous power supply, cooling, and running models in 15-minute bursts to avoid system strain. This initiative, Project Suncatcher, aims to develop large-scale orbital compute clusters, envisioning a future network of 81 interconnected satellites to process demanding AI workloads.

TechCrunch AI · Oct 1, 2026
Opus 5.5 loves to tell you ‘this matters’ (and other AI writing tells) Recommended

A new study by marketing firm Graphite has identified thousands of distinct linguistic patterns, or "tells," in AI-generated prose. Researchers found 13,000 phrases at least twice as common in AI content compared to human writing, with models like Claude Opus 5.5 frequently using "this matters" and OpenAI's Astra employing "may provide." These evolving yet persistent quirks allow humans to better distinguish between AI and human-authored text, highlighting the ongoing challenge for AI models to mimic natural human expression perfectly.

Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky believes current AI chatbots are unsuitable for e-commerce and travel planning, despite the company's recent rollout of AI-powered search. TechCrunch AI · Oct 1, 2026
Amazon releases its own Jev clone as decision models flood the web Recommended

Amazon Web Services has released Strands Decider 2B, an open-source decision model inspired by TypeSafe’s Jev, designed for high-speed, low-cost AI automation. This model sorts between pre-decided options and delivers confidence scores, built on the 'torso' of an LLM but optimized for calibrated choices rather than text generation. It offers a more reliable, lower-latency, and cost-effective alternative to fully featured LLMs for specific agentic workflow steps, addressing a growing demand for specialized computer intelligence.
