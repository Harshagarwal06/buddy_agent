Lightspeed is targeting $250 million for a new India-focused early-stage fund, Lightspeed India Partners V, specifically designed to invest in artificial intelligence startups. This new fund is half the size of its $500 million predecessor and has already secured 80% of its target, with plans to begin investing within two months over a two-and-a-half-year period. ElevenLabs’ CEO on margins, IPO timing, and telling customers they’re talking to a bot Must read

ElevenLabs, a four-year-old company specializing in text-to-speech AI, is now reportedly valued at $22 billion by its investors. The company is pacing at $600 million in annual recurring revenue (ARR), with over 55% generated from enterprise clients like Klarna and Deutsche Telekom, and the remainder from small businesses and creators. This significant valuation underscores strong investor confidence in its market position and growth trajectory, even as its CEO indicates a willingness to accept further margin compression to expand market share and pursue the goal of passing the Turing test for conversational AI.

Oracle sends force majeure notice on its New Mexico Stargate data center Recommended

Oracle has issued a force majeure notice to the developer of its Stargate data center campus in New Mexico, Project Jupiter. This contractual action allows Oracle to delay payments if the facility misses its 2028 operational target, stemming from significant energy supply challenges, including a delayed gas pipeline and pending air-quality permits. The notice underscores growing national scrutiny on AI data center construction and highlights the complex hurdles and potential for project delays faced by large-scale infrastructure initiatives, despite Oracle's stated commitment to the project.

Google Photos ‘Clueless’-inspired virtual closet is now available on Android and iOS Recommended

Google Photos has launched an AI-powered virtual closet feature, inspired by the movie "Clueless," now available to all users in the U.S., Brazil, and India on both iOS and Android devices. This technology uses artificial intelligence to scan users' outfit photos, creating a digital copy of their wardrobe that can be filtered by category and mixed to create new outfits. TechCrunch AI · Sep 24, 2026
PrismML brings its tiny LLMs to Qualcomm-powered smart glasses Recommended

PrismML, an AI Lab, has developed a version of its compact language models for smart glasses powered by Qualcomm's Snapdragon chips. The 1-bit Bonsai LLM, a 2-billion-parameter model, shrinks larger models by four times while retaining performance, enabling local processing on devices like the Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 Platform. This innovation allows smart glasses wearers to ask about what they are seeing in real-time, offering a privacy-focused alternative to cloud-dependent AI by utilizing on-device computing power.
