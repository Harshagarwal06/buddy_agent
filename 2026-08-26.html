TechCrunch AI · Aug 25, 2026
OpenAI’s Jalapeño chip is built for fast inference at scale, benchmarks show Recommended

OpenAI's Jalapeño chip is built for fast inference at scale, outperforming current state-of-the-art processors in benchmark tests. Jalapeño registers more tokens per user and more throughput per kilowatt than Nvidia's Blackwell system, serving more AI work per unit of power while returning responses quickly. This efficiency will allow OpenAI to serve a large number of customers with low latency, but the competition may advance significantly by the time Jalapeño reaches full deployment in 2027.

Accel-backed Keenable is indexing the web for AI agents Briefing

Keenable is building a web search index of over 100 billion documents for AI agents. This index allows AI chatbots to ground their responses with source documents, creating a new flywheel. Keenable's product is different from enterprise search solutions, as it innovates on how to narrow the search space based on the query, reducing the cost of serving and scanning the whole internet.
