The first American autonomous ground vehicles are fighting in Ukraine ★★★★★
TechCrunch AI  ·  2026-07-07

Forterra, a U.S. company, has deployed over 100 autonomous ground vehicles in Ukraine, marking the largest known use of such technology in combat by a U.S. defense tech firm.
