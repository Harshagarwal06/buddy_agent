Hackers can use 9 of the most popular AI tools to assemble massive botnets ★★★★★
Ars Technica AI  ·  2026-07-08

Researchers have developed a new type of AI-based attack called HalluSquatting, which allows hackers to assemble large botnets by exploiting prompt injection vulnerabilities in popular AI tools. This pull-based attack enables widespread, scalable infections by luring large language models to malicious websites, unlike previous push-based methods that were limited in scope. The technique poses a significant threat to AI security and could be used for large-scale cyberattacks like DDoS operations.
