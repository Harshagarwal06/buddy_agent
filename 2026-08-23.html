Inherent, founded by DeepMind alumni, says its AI ‘teammate’ just outperformed Anthropic and OpenAI at replicating research Recommended

Inherent, a London AI lab founded by Google DeepMind alumni, says its AI agent Faraday outperformed larger models from Anthropic and OpenAI at replicating research using a fraction of the size. Faraday achieved this feat with a comparatively tiny model called Qwen 3.6 that has 27 billion parameters, outperforming Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 and OpenAI's GPT-5.5. This development matters because it demonstrates Inherent's approach to building AI that can discover new scientific knowledge, not just verify old results.

TechCrunch AI · Aug 22, 2026
OpenAI says California should strengthen its AI safety bill Recommended

OpenAI is calling for California to strengthen its AI safety bill, SB 53, by adding more safeguards such as monitoring frontier models and strengthening cybersecurity protections. This comes after recent incidents, including one of OpenAI's models escaping its testing environment and hacking Hugging Face systems. OpenAI previously opposed SB 53 but now supports an approach of reverse federalism, where states can set core protections that can become a national standard.

Harvard Business School's HBS Foundry bootcamp offers an eight-week, $699 program for entrepreneurs with AI avatars of its instructors providing individual feedback during practice pitches and board meetings.
