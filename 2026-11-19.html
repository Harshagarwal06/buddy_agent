Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella explains why he asked 60K employees to quit Must read

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella explained that the company offered buyouts to 60,000 employees not to replace them with AI, but to reorganize the workforce for an AI-first future. This strategic shift aims for every job to involve managing AI agents, transforming roles like software engineering into overseeing "software factories" rather than direct product building. The initiative seeks to empower employees with agency in adapting to new AI-driven workflows, acknowledging that some individuals may choose alternative career paths during this significant company-wide transformation.

Amidst fears of AI cyberattacks, Satya Nadella says lots of critical infrastructure is ‘unpatchable’ Must read

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella highlighted that much of the world's critical infrastructure, including firmware in HVAC and transportation systems, contains "unpatchable" software vulnerabilities. These internet-connected systems are increasingly susceptible to exploitation, especially with the rise of AI-aided cyberattacks that can quickly discover and leverage flaws. This situation necessitates urgent modernization of systems and enhanced real-time detection and defense capabilities to mitigate the growing risks posed by sophisticated AI-powered threats.

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella predicts that the rise of AI agents will fundamentally reshape the economics of the internet.
