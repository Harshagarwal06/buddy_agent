Meta’s new Glimmer AI model offers a hint at Zuckerberg’s personal intelligence vision Must read

Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion parameter AI model designed for local AI agent deployment on consumer hardware, offering a glimpse into Mark Zuckerberg's vision of personal superintelligence. Glimmer supports multi-step tasks, text, and images, trained across 100 languages, and can operate on a user's device without cloud access. This development aims to empower individuals with AI capabilities, enabling personal agents to improve relationships, health, and careers, with the promise of free or affordable access to these tools.

Discovered Materials is playing AI whack-a-mole to hunt cooler chips Recommended

Discovered Materials is using AI to hunt for cooler chips by generating material leads and verifying candidate materials through simulations. The startup's software pipeline uses Anthropic models and foundational physics models to explore research directions and make thousands of guesses a day. This approach aims to address the thermal problems of semiconductor materials, which is a key challenge in reducing heat generation and improving dissipation in chips.
