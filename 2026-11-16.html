Following a suspected Chinese spy scandal involving former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, Congress is moving to tighten export rules on advanced AI chips. Both Democrats and Republicans support various measures, including renewed interest in bills like the Remote Access Security Act, and have demanded testimony from Nvidia leaders. Indian chip startup Bharat Semi lands $24M seed round as it eyes global AI sensor market Recommended

Indian semiconductor startup Bharat Semi has secured $24 million in seed funding to develop AI-enabled image sensors for global markets. These programmable sensors integrate image capture, processing, and artificial intelligence capabilities onto a single chip, aiming to reduce system costs by at least 70% compared to conventional setups using separate components. This integrated approach could enable more efficient and cost-effective AI applications in smart homes, industrial automation, and aerospace by minimizing the need for additional, expensive computing hardware.

TechCrunch AI · Nov 16, 2026
Apple launches new app to study how people use AI for health Recommended

Apple has launched a new study app in partnership with healthcare AI company OpenEvidence to understand how people use AI for health. The app allows iPhone 15 or later users with Apple Intelligence to ask health questions, receiving answers grounded in clinical data from sources like The New England Journal of Medicine. Participants can rate responses and share HealthKit data, with all information anonymized and deleted after 24 hours. This initiative aims to gather insights into how clinically grounded AI can support individuals' health journeys and inform the development of trusted future health tools.

The Verge AI · Nov 16, 2026
Apple’s new study looks to improve the effectiveness of AI health tools Recommended

Apple has launched a new study in collaboration with OpenEvidence to understand how people interact with and evaluate conversational AI health tools. Participants in the US will use a new iOS app to ask medical questions, receive responses based on peer-reviewed sources, and then rate them for accuracy, detail, and relevance, with an option to share personal HealthKit data for contextualized answers. This initiative aims to improve the design and functionality of AI-powered health tools, ensuring they deliver more relevant, understandable, and actionable health information to users.
