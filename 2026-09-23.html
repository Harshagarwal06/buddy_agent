Qualcomm launches two new smartphone chips with emphasis on AI Must read

Qualcomm has introduced two new flagship smartphone processors, Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 and Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, designed to enhance on-device artificial intelligence capabilities. These chips feature new sensing hubs capable of running small AI models locally, with the Extreme version supporting a 30-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model, surpassing Apple's recent 20-billion-parameter offering. This development enables improved personalization for AI agents, local personal scribes, better task automation suggestions, and advanced camera functions like pixel-level control and 8K 60fps video recording.

TechCrunch AI · Sep 22, 2026
AstroForge is putting AI in command of its next spacecraft Must read

AstroForge is developing an AI-powered autonomous control system for its next spacecraft, aiming to reduce reliance on extensive ground teams. This in-house transformer-based model, named "Solo," is designed to enable the spacecraft to self-diagnose and solve problems onboard, addressing communication challenges faced in previous deep-space missions. By shifting control from Earth to the spacecraft itself, AstroForge seeks to make deep-space exploration more cost-effective and accessible for startups, potentially revolutionizing mission operations.

Meta admits Muse’s likeness to OpenClaw isn’t a coincidence Recommended

Meta's Muse AI product is confirmed to be heavily inspired by the open-source OpenClaw AI, following user speculation about their likeness. Nat Friedman, Meta's head of product, stated Muse was "built from scratch" but "heavily inspired," acknowledging that specific file names and content, including the SOUL.md configuration, were nearly identical because OpenClaw "got those things exactly right." This revelation underscores Meta's strategy of adopting successful product features for its own scalable consumer offerings, echoing its past replication of Snapchat's stories format.
