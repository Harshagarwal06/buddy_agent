How RAG works in buddy_agent

Two paths through the news archive: a vector-based semantic search that actually powers retrieval-augmented generation, and a plain SQL keyword search kept as a fast, exact-match fallback.

Why keep both paths
Keyword search is fast and exact for known terms; semantic search finds related coverage even when the wording is different.
