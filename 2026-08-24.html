Show HN: Writing-eval, local deterministic style checks for AI-written drafts Recommended

Writing-eval is a tool for local deterministic style checks of AI-written drafts. It allows teams to measure a draft against a chosen editorial voice by building a reusable style profile from reference prose and checking a draft against it. Woe Is Em: The Sad Lifecycle of an AI Tell Recommended

ChatGPT's overuse of the em dash became a stylistic 'tell' of AI-generated prose in 2025, causing writers to fear association with AI. Professional writers and ordinary people used 'humanizer' tools to eliminate em dashes and other signs of AI, but OpenAI eventually addressed the issue by allowing users to customize ChatGPT's em dash usage. As a result, the em dash is nearing the end of its lifecycle as a ChatGPT tell, leaving a cautionary tale about the consequences of AI-generated content.
