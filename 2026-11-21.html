This hypothesis, proposed in 1859, concerns the distribution of prime numbers and is one of seven Millennium Prize problems, carrying a $1 million bounty if verified. TechCrunch AI · Nov 20, 2026
Chips can survive the space environment — can their financing? Recommended

Space data centers face significant financing hurdles because the chips used in orbit cannot serve as collateral for loans, unlike their terrestrial counterparts. Current data center financing models depend on lenders being able to repossess chips if a borrower defaults, a safeguard that reduces borrowing costs. This inability to secure loans with unrecoverable orbital assets could make funding space-based compute infrastructure more expensive and challenging, potentially slowing its development despite future cost predictions.
