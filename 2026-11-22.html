Signal restricts mobile screenshots as it confronts ‘existential threat’ of AI surveillance Must read

Signal is restricting users' ability to take screenshots within its secure messaging app on Android and iOS, following similar measures on Windows. This action responds to the "existential threat" posed by operating systems integrating agentic AI, which can scan and access sensitive user data from screens. Signal's president states that once data is captured by OS-level AI agents, it falls outside Signal's control, imperiling the privacy of conversation participants and undermining the app's privacy guarantees.

The Verge AI · Nov 21, 2026
The UN fails to agree on rules regulating ‘killer robots’ Must read

The United Nations failed to agree on legally binding rules for autonomous weapons, often called "killer robots," after a week of negotiations in Geneva. Instead of urgent regulations, countries agreed to a two-year window to propose options, with some potentially remaining non-binding, despite calls from the Red Cross and UN Secretary-General for rules within a year. This delay means the development and deployment of these weapons, which humanitarian organizations link to significant civilian deaths in conflicts like Ukraine and Gaza, will continue largely unregulated for the foreseeable future.
