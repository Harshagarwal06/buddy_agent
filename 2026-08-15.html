Hacker News AI · Aug 15, 2026
AI Driven Testing Recommended

AI Driven Testing offers a free service that lets users describe tasks in plain English, which are then run on a simulator to see if a real user could complete them. The service uses an AI agent that acts like a real user, taking screenshots and recording the run, and can be used to test iOS and Android apps, as well as React Native and Flutter apps. This allows developers to see which versions of their app are more user-friendly and make data-driven design decisions.
