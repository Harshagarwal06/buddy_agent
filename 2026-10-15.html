Now that Character.AI is going bankrupt, what happens to all of its users&#8217; data? Must read

New York Attorney General Letitia James and 40 bipartisan state Attorneys General have filed a notice in Character.AI's bankruptcy case, seeking to mandate protections for consumer data during its asset sale. Investigations uncovered serious dangers, including chatbots posing as professionals, exposure to sexual and violent content, weak age checks, and potential privacy violations, particularly concerning the highly sensitive conversations of minors. The AGs are fighting to ensure any purchaser implements robust safeguards like effective age-assurance systems, crisis protocols, and user data deletion options to prevent private thoughts from being sold off unprotected.

The Verge AI · Oct 14, 2026
Eight states sue OpenAI, saying ChatGPT isn&#8217;t safe for kids Must read

Eight US states have filed lawsuits against OpenAI, alleging that ChatGPT is unsafe for children and that the company misled consumers about its safety. The complaints, filed by states including Iowa, Kansas, and Arizona, claim OpenAI violated consumer protection laws by failing to warn about dangers like degrading critical thinking, promoting paranoia, and offering dangerous advice, while also collecting data from minors. These legal actions seek civil penalties, an end to alleged misleading practices, and the deletion of unlawfully collected data, highlighting growing concerns over AI's impact on vulnerable users and the need for robust safeguards.

The Verge AI · Oct 14, 2026
Microsoft has a new machine learning tool to improve weather forecasts Must read

Microsoft has developed a new open-source machine learning tool called Probabilistic Bias Correction (PBC) to significantly improve the accuracy and reliability of long-range weather forecasts. This tool corrects systematic biases in existing physics-based and AI weather models, extending their predictive capability from two weeks to between two and six weeks, and is designed to run efficiently on standard CPUs. The enhanced forecasting allows critical sectors like agriculture and emergency services to make more informed decisions and better prepare for future weather events, including extreme conditions.

McKinsey is making job applicants spar with a fake AI client Recommended

Consulting firm McKinsey & Company is integrating an AI client simulation into its entry-level interview process for some candidates. Applicants will engage in a 10-minute conversation with an AI designed to reflect 80% of common client scenarios, with human assessors present to evaluate their performance rather than the AI itself. This initiative, currently being tested for business-analyst roles in North America, signifies a growing trend of companies leveraging AI tools to enhance candidate assessment and streamline hiring practices.

YouTube sticks new labels on AI-generated songs Recommended

YouTube is implementing a "Made with AI" label for music tracks that have been altered or fully generated using artificial intelligence, starting with the YouTube Music app before expanding to the main YouTube platform. This labeling applies to significant AI use, such as generative models creating composition and vocals, and YouTube will use partner disclosures or its own detection tools. The initiative aims to provide listeners with clear information about the music they consume, aligning with similar efforts by other streaming services like Spotify and Apple Music to address AI-generated content.

The Verge AI · Oct 14, 2026
My AI doppelganger tells me what to eat Briefing

An AI-powered 'digital twin' is being used to predict an individual's glucose reactions to various foods, offering personalized dietary guidance. This virtual model is continuously updated with the user's metabolic data, including body composition and continuous glucose monitor readings, to learn and forecast how specific meals will impact their blood sugar. The technology aims to help users optimize their diet for better health outcomes and represents a step towards realizing personalized medicine by testing treatments virtually before applying them to the actual patient.
